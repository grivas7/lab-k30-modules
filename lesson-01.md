## 2. Classroom Flow (60 Minutes)
* **00–15 min | Meet Your Machine:**
  * Open the lid with two hands from the center.
  * Find `Power`, `Left Click`, `Space`, `Enter`, `Backspace`, and the 4 `Arrow Keys`.
  * Mini-game: *Keyboard Detective* — "Find the first letter of your name! Press Space! Press Enter!"
* **15–25 min | Screen-Free Game ("I Am Karel"):**
  * Teacher acts as Karel on floor tiles. Students give exact commands in English: `MOVE`, `TURN LEFT`.
  * Rule: If a command is unclear, Karel freezes with a funny beep.
* **25–50 min | First Steps with Karel on Laptop:**
  * **Explorer (Ages 5–7):** Move Karel 3 steps forward to the green goal using Arrow keys / visual blocks.
  * **Builder (Ages 8+):** Type `move();` in the editor to guide Karel along a 5-step hallway.
* **50–60 min | Clean Shut Down:**
  * Close the browser, click `Start -> Power -> Shut down`, and close the laptop lid gently.
