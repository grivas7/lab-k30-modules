# LAB-K30 – Lesson 01
## Meet Your Machine & Meet Karel

**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Age:** 5–9 years  
**Level:** Beginner

### 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, children will be able to:

- Identify the basic parts of a computer.
- Recognize the keyboard and mouse.
- Identify the Arrow keys, Enter and Space.
- Explain what a robot is.
- Understand that a robot follows commands.
- Give Karel his first movement instructions.

### 3. Video – Meet Karel

Duration: 1–2 minutes.

Video script:

Hello, little explorers!

Welcome to LAB-K30!

My name is Karel. I am a little robot.

I love adventures, but I need your help!

I cannot decide where to go by myself. I need commands.

Can you help me move forward?

Let's start our first adventure!

### 4. Explorer Challenge – Ages 5–7

Objective: Learn basic computer controls and visual directions.

Activities:

1. Find the mouse.
2. Practice Left Click.
3. Find the Arrow keys.
4. Point to the Up Arrow.
5. Help Karel move three steps forward.

Success criteria: The child can identify the basic controls and guide Karel using visual instructions.

### 5. Builder Challenge – Ages 8+

Objective: Understand the relationship between commands and robot movement.

Example:

```text
move()
move()
move()
```

Explain that each command tells Karel to move one step forward.

Challenge: Write five movement commands and guide Karel through a simple path without hitting a wall.

Success criteria: The child understands that commands must be written in the correct order.

### 6. Teacher Questions

- What is a computer?
- What is a robot?
- Can a robot understand our thoughts?
- What is a command?
- What happens if we give the wrong command?

### 7. Final Quiz

1. What does a robot need to move?
2. Which key moves the cursor or selection upward in a keyboard-controlled activity?
3. What does `move()` tell Karel to do?

### 8. Homework – Optional

Draw your own robot.

Give your robot a name and write three commands for its first adventure.

### 9. Teacher Evaluation

- Identifies computer controls.
- Understands basic robot commands.
- Completes the first Karel mission.
- Participates actively.

**Lesson complete: My First Robot Mission!**
